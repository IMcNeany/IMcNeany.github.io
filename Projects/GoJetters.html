Overview

Go Jetters: Cadet Rescue is a educational adventure game for 4-6 year olds based on the Cbeebies show Go Jetters. Players explore weather-themed locations collecting coins, completing mini-games and learning about the weather.

I joined the company towards the end of production, pickingup up the PlayCanvas engine and quickly onboarded with the team. I tested each mini-game providing clear and concise reproduction steps for any issues found. As the team working on the project was small I was able to get involved debugging and fixing issue which allowed me to gain familiarity with codebase and PlayCanvas.

Contributions

  • Tested mini-games and reported issues with clear reproduction steps
  • Debugged and fixed reported issues
