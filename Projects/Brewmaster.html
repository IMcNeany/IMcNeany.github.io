Brewmaster: Beer Brewing Simulator

Software used:

Unreal 4, Asana, Steamworks

Languages:

C++

Developer:

Auroch Digital

Brewmaster: Beer Brewing Simulator is a game aimed at beer enthusiast and cozy gamers.

The aim for Brewmaster was to be as realistic of a beer brewing as possible, I contributed to the development of the simulation. I collaborated closely with the artist to develop a system to feed the brewing simulator data into the liquid shader to create a variety of different effects from the data fed in. I worked on creating a scalable and reusable Jobs and Competition system, including adding in UI for them that is shown in the two in game magazines that would allow design to just add data to create new jobs and competitions. Working on hooking up a variety of UI and working closely with the UI artist to make the taste card screens that are used to portray the scoring of the beer as well as the in game magazines look and feel real. I also added the system to choose beer bottles and glass types to display it in. I also assisted with the deployment of builds on the steam backend. I also got involved with the marketing campaign, appearing in a video and an article as well a speaking in person at a Bristol Games Hub meetup.

Contributions

  • Close collaboration with the artists to create stunning visuals
  • Create a scalable and reusable Jobs and Competition system
  • Close collaboration with the UI team to create resposive and interactive UI
