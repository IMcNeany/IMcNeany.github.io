V Rising

Software used:

Unity, PS5 Developer Tools, Jira, Asana

Languages:

C#

Developer:

Auroch Digital, Stunlock Studios

V Rising is an open world surival game. You play as a vampire who needs to avoid the sunlight, while building up your base, levelling up equipment and defeating bosses.

We developed the Playstation 5 version of the game, where I contributed to adapting and implementing UI screen based on updated designs, ensuring that were compatiable with gamepad controls. These were then later also applied to the PC version of the game. I also worked on optimsations using the PS5 Developement tools to identify the performance bottlenecks. I then implemented solutions to address the bottlenecks to increase overall performance and stability of the game.

Contributions

  • Implmenting UI screens with controller support
  • Focusing on performance and optimisation
