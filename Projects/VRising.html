Overview

V Rising is an open world survival game by Stunlock Studios. Players are a vampire who must avoid sunlight while hunting for blood, while building up a base, crafting gear and defeating bosses.

The work involved porting the PC version in to a PlayStation 5 version of the game. I adapted and implemented UI screens to updated designs built for gamepad, ensuring full it worked with both controller and keyboard. These changes were then later brought over to the PC version of the game. I also used PS5 development tools and the Unity Profiler to identify performance bottlenecks and then implemented fixes to increase performance and stability of the game.

Contributions

  • Implemented UI screens to updated designs with gamepad support
  • Implemented optimisations that improved performance and stability
