Overview

Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun - Words of Vengeance is a fast-paced typing game spin-off of the retro FPS Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun. Players fight their way through hordes of enemies by typing words to shoot them.

I prototyped and implemented the first iteration of the game's core typing mechanic including, handling certain input behaviour such as ignoring keys that don't count towards words e.g. the Space key. I then integrated the typing mechanic with the enemy damage system and the UI to create the core gameplay loop and give players the immediate feedback on each keystroke.
