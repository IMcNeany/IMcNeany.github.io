Overview

Warhammer Survivors is a survivors-like action roguelite, set across the Warhammer 40,000 and Age of Sigmar universes. Players choose a character and battle hordes of enemies, upgrading weapons, fighting bosses and collecting experience to level up.

I worked prototyping some of the game's weapons, iterating closely with the design team to refine how each one played. I also implemented several pickups along with their effects, relics along with other content, along with quality of life improvements and bug fixes.

Contributions

  • Prototyped weapons and iterated on with the design team
  • Implementing pickups and their effects
