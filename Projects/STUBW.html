Overview

Starship Troopers: Ultimate Bug War! is a retro-style first-person shooter based on the 1997 film Starship Troopers. The game has a split of human levels (where you fight against bugs) and bug levels (where you fight the humans).

I joined the team with a year of production left and started by building developer tooling using Python with Unreal Editor tools to aid with workflows. I automated sprite importing and setup, added map validations and the creation and setup of placeholder voice lines from dialogue with text-to-speech software. Towards the end of the project the focus shifted towards stability and optimisation. This involved profiling the game on different platforms, identifying CPU and GPU bottlenecks in general gameplay and any intensive parts of levels and then implementing fixes to address them.

Contributions
